Welcome to SalahTracker!

A simple, daily companion to help you stay consistent with your prayers.

  • • Log Daily: Tap prayers on the "Today" tab to mark them complete.
  • • Build Streaks: Complete all 5 daily to watch your fire streak grow.
  • • Review History: Use the "Calendar" to track stats or click past dates to edit entries.
  • • Saved Automatically: It will remember your entries locally so they won't disappear.
